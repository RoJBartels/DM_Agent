# D&D 5e SRD — Core Adjudication (excerpt)

## Ability Checks

An ability check tests a character's talent and training in an effort to overcome a challenge.
The DM decides which of the six abilities is relevant and sets a Difficulty Class (DC). To make
a check, roll a d20 and add the relevant ability modifier. If the total equals or exceeds the
DC, the check succeeds.

Typical DCs: Very easy 5, Easy 10, Medium 15, Hard 20, Very hard 25, Nearly impossible 30.

## The Six Abilities and Their Skills

- **Strength** — Athletics (climbing, jumping, swimming, grappling).
- **Dexterity** — Acrobatics (balance, tumbling), Sleight of Hand (palming, lockpicking by
  feel), Stealth (hiding, moving silently, sneaking past a guard).
- **Constitution** — raw endurance; rarely has skills, used for concentration and exhaustion saves.
- **Intelligence** — Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion.
- **Wisdom** — Animal Handling, Insight (reading intentions, detecting lies), Medicine,
  Perception (spotting, hearing, noticing), Survival (tracking, foraging).
- **Charisma** — Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion.

When a character is proficient in a skill relevant to a check, add the proficiency bonus.

## Saving Throws

A saving throw represents resisting a threat: a spell, a trap, a poison. Roll a d20, add the
relevant ability modifier (and proficiency if proficient in that save), and compare to the DC.
Dexterity saves dodge area effects; Constitution saves resist poison and maintain concentration;
Wisdom saves resist charm and fear.

## Advantage and Disadvantage

When you have advantage, roll two d20s and use the higher. With disadvantage, roll two and use
the lower. Favorable circumstances (help, high ground, a clever plan) grant advantage;
unfavorable ones (poor visibility, restraint, exhaustion) impose disadvantage.

## Attacks and Damage

To attack, roll a d20 and add your attack bonus; if it meets or beats the target's Armor Class
(AC), it hits. On a hit, roll the weapon's damage dice. A natural 20 is a critical hit — roll
the damage dice twice. A natural 1 always misses.

## Conditions (selected)

- **Prone** — melee attacks against you have advantage; ranged have disadvantage.
- **Grappled** — speed becomes 0.
- **Frightened** — disadvantage on checks and attacks while the source is in sight.
- **Poisoned** — disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.
