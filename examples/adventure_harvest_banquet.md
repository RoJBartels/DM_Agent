# The Harvest Banquet — a short adventure for Aldenmoor

An advisory outline for a Dungeon Master running a game set in the Barony of
Aldenmoor. It sketches an intended arc; the party is free to ignore any of it.

## Act 1 — Arrival at Vane Hall

The party reaches the town of Muireach on the eve of Duke Aldric Vane's Harvest
Banquet. The streets are tense: river-guild men and lamplighters of the Order of
the Drowned Lantern eye each other across the wharves. The party is expected at
the hall to renew oaths.

> Read aloud: "The black timbers of Vane Hall rise over Muireach's slate roofs,
> every window lit against the moor's drizzle. At the gate, a steward checks your
> names against a long list — and behind him, two lamplighters watch you closely."

Trigger: the party arrives in Muireach or approaches Vane Hall.

## Act 2 — The Banquet Interrupted

During the banquet, Lady Maren Ashcroft makes a veiled accusation against the
Duke about the barrow beneath the hall. If the party investigates, they can find
signs that the Order has been digging in the cellars. The author hopes the party
uncovers the connection between Maren and the Order here — but they may side with
her, expose her, or ignore the whole thing.

Trigger: the party attends the banquet, or begins asking about Lady Maren or the
cellars.

## Act 3 — The Barrow Beneath

The digging breaks through into the sealed moor-clan barrow under Vane Hall.
Whatever the Order has been seeking is here. The intended climax is a confrontation
in the barrow — Duke, Maren, and the Order all want different things from it — but
the party decides who they help, who they oppose, and whether the barrow is opened
at all.

Trigger: someone breaches the barrow, or the party descends into Vane Hall's
cellars.
