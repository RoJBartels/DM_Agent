# The Barony of Aldenmoor

Aldenmoor is a rain-soaked barony of peat moors and drowned roads, ruled for thirty years
from the black-timbered hall of **Duke Aldric Vane**. The Duke is an aging, careful man who
keeps the peace between the river guilds and the old moor-clans. Every autumn he hosts the
**Harvest Banquet** in Vane Hall, where every noble house of the moor is expected to attend
and renew their oaths. This year's banquet is only days away.

## Vane Hall

Vane Hall stands on the one dry hill above the town of **Muireach**, a huddle of slate roofs
where the river guilds keep their warehouses. The hall is old, its cellars older still —
locals say the foundations were laid by the moor-clans long before the Vanes came.

## The Order of the Drowned Lantern

The **Order of the Drowned Lantern** is a secretive society of lamplighters and ferrymen who
claim to guide lost souls across the moors. They are outwardly pious, but the Order keeps a
secret: they know the true, unmarked location of the old moor-clan barrow beneath Vane Hall,
and they have been quietly digging toward it for years. The Order and the river guilds despise
each other — the guilds see the lamplighters as thieves and grave-robbers.

## Lady Maren Ashcroft

**Lady Maren Ashcroft** is the sharpest of the moor-clan nobles and the Duke's most likely
rival. She resents that her clan's ancestral barrow lies under a Vane hall, and she has been
seen meeting quietly with members of the Order of the Drowned Lantern. Whether she means to
reclaim the barrow or seize the duchy outright, no one can say.

## The Moor-Clan Barrow

Beneath Vane Hall lies an unopened **moor-clan barrow**, sealed for centuries. The moor-clans
hold it sacred; the Order of the Drowned Lantern covets whatever is buried within. The Duke
does not know it is there.
